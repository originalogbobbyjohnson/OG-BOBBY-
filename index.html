THE LEVELS

    ABOUT THE GAME

    The Brooklyn Music Game

    A true Bay Ridge story told in songs. Every song is a level, and the story only moves when you prove you were listening.

    • Listen. Each level is a chapter: the block, the fights, Wall Street, the comeback.
    • Answer. The song stops and asks what you just heard. Miss it and you get a new question, not a do-over.
    • Unlock. Get a song right and it's yours to play in full.
    • Earn Street Cred. First-try answers fill your meter. Climb from New Face to Corner Kid, OG Soldier and OG Protégé.
    • Collect. Finish any block of 3 songs to earn that block's playlist. Finish all 9 to earn Volume 1.
    • Run it back. Earned a rank? Replay your unlocked songs with new questions, just for fun. No rewards, just bragging rights.
    • Raw Cuts. Stripped-down versions of the songs, in their own room.
    • Road to '99. Volume 2 comes next. Both volumes make the '99 album, and the climb goes on to OG Certified.
