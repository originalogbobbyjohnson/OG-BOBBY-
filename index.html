    ABOUT THE GAME

    The Brooklyn Music Game

    A true Bay Ridge story told in songs. Every song is a level, and the story only moves when you prove you were listening.

    • Listen. Each episode is a chapter: the block, the fights, Wall Street, the comeback.
    • Answer. The song stops and asks what you just heard. Miss it and you get a new question, not a do-over.
    • Unlock. Get a song right and it's yours to play in full.
    • Earn Street Cred. First-try answers fill your meter. Climb from New Face to Corner Kid, OG Soldier and OG Protégé.
    • Stack Juice. Every right answer and star adds Juice. A perfect Season 1 is 9,900. Hot streaks stack Flames toward the ON FIRE badge, and daily questions stack Daily Wins toward the REGULAR badge. Both live in your trophy case. Stack enough and you go Gold, then Platinum, Multi-Platinum and Diamond.
    • Collect. Finish any block of 3 songs to earn that block's playlist. Finish all 9 to earn Season 1.
    • Run it back. Earned a rank? Replay your unlocked songs with new questions, just for fun. No rewards, just bragging rights.
    • Director’s Cuts. Stripped-down versions of the songs, in their own room.
    • Road to '99. OG Made is the music series: Season 1 ('9) and Season 2 (9), nine episodes each. Put them together and you get '99. The climb goes on to OG Certified.
